**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Status:** ACCEPTED — SELECTED FOR SCHEMATIC (K1 PRIMARY, K2 FOOTPRINT PROVISION, K3 REJECTED)

**Rationale (why K1):**
- **Leakage is the gate** — REQ-DUT-001 + DEC-030 require ≤10 pA at DUT for 1 GΩ@0.5 V to hold 2% raw (0.5% cal). Only K1/K2 meet it; K3 fails by design (INA inputs are BJT, not JFET/fA). OPA140 Ib 10 pA max is the only affordable path that also meets Vos/TC/noise for ±200 µV @0 V after cal. INA116 passes Ib but its Vos/TC/BW/cost are strictly worse than K1 for a G=1 diff — no reason to pay $22 for larger offset.
- **CMRR adequate after cal** — K1 54 dB gives worst 4 mV @2 V CM (0.2% @2 V, 4% @0.1 V). ReRAM DUT voltage is differential only (SENSE_HI−SENSE_LO), not CM-dependent beyond CM error. With single-point gain cal at +2 V plus software correction (Vdut = G·(Vhi−Vlo)+ Vos), residual CM error <0.5 mV over 0–30 °C in lab (verified by LTspice CM sweep ±2 V in gate E). If CMRR proves limiting in prototype, LT5400 footprint (K2) upgrades to 86 dB without re-spin — same netlist, better resistor.
- **Simplest = fewest precision dependencies** — K1 uses 3× OPA140 (one BOM line, dual OPA2140 option) + 4 standard 0805 0.1% resistors. No matched array sourcing risk, no INA supply sequencing. R5.1 transient already validated K1 pole (1 kΩ+15 pF ≈10.6 MHz) with vendor LT1970: stable at +2/−2/+0.1 V, 100 pF–1 nF, R_iso 47 Ω, overshoot <0.01% @2 V, 44%→8 µs @0.1 V (slew mitigation documented). K2/K3 would require re-sim but add no stability benefit (same OPA140 loop).
- **Traceability:** K1 directly implements KELVIN_SENSE_ARCHITECTURE §1 Fig.1 (high-Z buffer ≥10 GΩ before any divider, filter after buffer, SENSE encloses DUT only). V_FORCE = V_DUT + I·R_LEAD + V_SHUNT (canonical) — headroom still budgeted on FORCE.

**Consequences:**
- Schematic 04_KELVIN_SENSE captures **K1 PRIMARY**: SENSE_HI → U401A OPA140 (follower, 100 nF+10 µF decoupling, 10 Ω series + 5 pF Cin at DUT) → R301/R302 + U402 OPA140 diff → node VDIFF → LT1970 −IN. SENSE_LO → U401B → R303/R304 → same diff. Diff gain =1 (10 kΩ/10 kΩ). Provision U403 LT5400 footprint overlapping R301–R304 (DNP K1 / FIT K2). Test points TP_SENSE_HI_BUF, TP_SENSE_LO_BUF, TP_DIFF.
- **Open-sense reed isolation** (DEC-029): reeds K301/K302 Coto 9007 (<1 pA, 1 pF Coff) in series with SENSE_HI/LO **after** DUT but **before** buffer? No — buffer input leakage dominates, so reeds **disconnect the weak pull/test network only**, not the sense path (per IR-03 switched continuity). Implementation: weak 10 MΩ pull (R306/R307 10 MΩ to V_FORCE/2 divider) behind NO contacts of K301/K302 → when OUTPUT_ENABLE low, reeds CLOSED for continuity test (window comparator U404 TLV3501 on |VDIFF−V_FORCE| >0.5 V flags OPEN), firmware logs, then reeds OPEN before OUTPUT ON, latch via SR (U405) with **latch OFF** behavior: any OPEN flag or watchdog/POR latches fallback to FORCE mode (SENSE shorted to FORCE divider via analog switch ADG1419 housekeeping, not precision path) until explicit SENS:REM ON + OUTPUT cycle. During valid measurement, reeds OPEN → DUT sees only OPA140 Cin 5 pF, leakage <1 pA (reed Coff), no DC load.
- FILTER provision stays in 03_OUTPUT_STAGE (LT1970 FILTER to SENSE− via C_FILTER 0805 DNP 1 nF–100 nF, default DNP/open baseline, 1 kΩ internal per 1970afc), not in Kelvin sheet.
- LTspice gate E: K1 netlist (OPAx140.LIB) + vendor LT1970.sub re-sim at +0.1 V, +2 V, −2 V, 100 pF/1 nF, R_iso 47 Ω, both CV (10 kΩ) and CC (100 Ω for +2 V@10 mA limit, 1 kΩ for +2 V@50 µA) — see simulation/phase3/vendor_lt1970_R5p1E/* and results.
- BOM: OPA140AID x3 (or OPA2140 dual + single), LT5400 DNP, Coto 9007 x2, TLV3501, SR latch (SN74LVC1G74).
